THE PROBLEM

“We tried LinkedIn ads. They don’t work.”

I hear it every week. It’s almost never the channel. It’s how the channel is being run.

“We asked for demos from people who’d never heard of us.”

Cold audience, hot ask. Nobody books a call with a logo they’ve never seen.

“Our cost per lead looks great. Sales says the leads are junk.”

The campaigns are chasing cheap clicks, not the accounts you actually want to sell to.

“We launched, and then nothing really changed.”

Same ads for months, no testing, a narrow audience that stopped paying attention long ago.

“Leadership asks what LinkedIn brings in. We can’t answer.”

You’re measuring last-click on a channel that does its work before the click.

WHAT I BELIEVE

LinkedIn builds influence. Pipeline follows.

Most of your future customers aren’t buying today. But they’re on LinkedIn, and it’s the only place you can reach them by exact company, role and seniority, long before they start searching.

Show up there consistently with something worth their attention, and when the need arrives, they don’t search for “a solution”. They search for you.

Search captures demand. LinkedIn creates it.

HOW I WORK

Fix what’s there before building what’s new.

Most agencies start by launching new campaigns. I start by understanding what you already have, because the fastest wins are usually hiding in your current setup.

1. Investigate

I dig into your ad account, tracking, CRM, positioning and sales conversations. Which accounts matter? What are they seeing today? Where is budget leaking?

2. Improve

Before anything new, I fix the foundations: targeting, account lists, tracking, campaign structure and the ads already running. This alone often frees up budget and sharpens results.

3. Ideate & create

With solid foundations, we build what’s new: fresh angles, company and thought-leader ads, structured creative tests. Then we keep what works, cut what doesn’t, and scale into pipeline.
